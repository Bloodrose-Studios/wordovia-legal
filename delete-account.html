Delete Your Account & Data

Wordovia — Bloodrose Studios

Wordovia can be played entirely without an account. If you never linked a Google account in Settings, no personal data leaves your device, and uninstalling the app deletes everything.

If you did link a Google account (for cloud save sync across devices), the following describes how to remove that account and request deletion of the associated data.

Step 1: Unlink in-app (removes future syncing immediately)

  1. Open Wordovia
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Under Account, tap Unlink Google Account

This immediately signs you out and stops any further data from being synced to our servers. Your local progress on this device is not affected.

Step 2: Request deletion of your cloud data

Unlinking does not automatically erase the save that was already stored in our database. To request that it be deleted, email us at bloodrosestudiosofficial@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my Wordovia data" and include:

  • The Google account email you used to link Wordovia (so we can find the right record)
  • Confirmation that you want your cloud save permanently deleted

What gets deleted

  • Deleted: your cloud-saved game progress, currency, and settings tied to your account (stored in our Firestore database under your account ID)
  • Not affected: any progress saved locally on your device (uninstall the app to remove that separately)
  • Retention: we process deletion requests manually and aim to complete them within 30 days of your email

Other data

If you previously submitted a support ticket through the in-app Contact Support form, you can request that it be deleted the same way — include "and my support tickets" in your email.
