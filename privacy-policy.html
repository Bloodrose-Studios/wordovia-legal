Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Wordovia ("we", "our", or "the game") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

Game Progress & Preferences
We store your level completions, in-game currency (coins, gems), powerup inventories, and settings locally on your device using SharedPreferences. If you choose to link a Google account, this data is additionally synced to Firebase Firestore so it is available across your devices.

Account Information
If you link a Google account, we store the associated email address alongside your synced progress. This is used solely to let our support team locate and act on your account if you request its deletion (see the account deletion page linked from our store listing); it is not used for marketing and is never shared with third parties.

Device Information
We may collect anonymised device identifiers and technical data (operating system, app version) for crash reporting and performance monitoring.

Advertising
Wordovia displays rewarded advertisements via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID) and usage data to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalised ads through your device settings. For more details see Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics
We use Firebase Analytics to understand how players interact with the game (e.g. which levels are played most). This data is aggregated and anonymised. No personally identifiable information is included.

How We Use Your Data

  • Save and restore your game progress
  • Personalise in-game rewards and offers
  • Improve game balance and user experience
  • Display relevant in-game advertisements

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the third-party service providers listed above (Google Firebase, Google AdMob) as necessary to operate the game, and only under appropriate data processing agreements.

Data Retention

Local game data is retained until you uninstall the app or manually reset your progress in Settings → Data. Cloud-synced data can be deleted by unlinking your Google account and contacting us at bloodrosestudiosofficial@gmail.com.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Please contact bloodrosestudiosofficial@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

Children

Wordovia is intended for players aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can remove it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
